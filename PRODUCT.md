# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React, TypeScript, and Vite were selected for a maintainable, fast frontend with tested interactions and no unnecessary server framework.

## Users

Couples, families, and friend groups evaluating a private seaside escape or celebration venue near Davao.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors discover Gem by the Sea, understand its private-resort experience, trust it through supplied guest feedback, and contact the property to plan a stay.

## Positioning

A spacious hidden paradise in Samal where a private beach, expansive property, and memorable infinity-pool setting make group escapes feel personal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Frontend-only marketing page with a non-confirming stay preview. No live pricing, availability, payment, operating-hours, or reservation claims. Direct phone contact is the conversion path.

## Brand Commitments

The business name is Gem by the Sea. The requested identity is vibrant, inspiring, destination-led, and built around aurora gradients and tropical color.

## Evidence on Hand

Supplied facts include a 4.5 rating from 27 reviews, the address 4Q6V+4JH, Aundanao, Samal, Davao del Norte, the phone number 0922 882 1234, and three named review themes. No approved logo, owned photography, pricing, or live booking system was supplied.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the feeling of privacy and place.
- Use real supplied proof rather than invented commercial claims.
- Make the path to direct contact unmistakable.
- Keep trip planning useful without implying a confirmed booking.
- Preserve an excellent experience across mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operability, visible focus, touch-friendly controls, semantic structure, and reduced-motion support.
